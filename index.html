Directions / how to reach

Getting here is easy!

Our celebrations are in Madhavam, Malviya Nagar, Jaipur . Whether you arrive by car or metro, please check the live route to your particular venue before setting off.

🚗 By car or cab ⌄

From Delhi, Jaipur, or either airport, take a cab or drive towards Malviya Nagar / Patrika Gate (famous tourist point) . Traffic and travel time vary, so use the venue’s Google Maps directions for the most current route.

✈️ From Delhi Airport ⌄

From either Terminal 1 or Terminal 3, take the cab or metro to Dhaula Kuan for direct buses to Jaipur or can take metro to NDLS metro station . At NDLS Metro station, move to NDLS railway staion and board a train to Jaipur. . Get off at Jaipur bus stop or railway station , and take a local cab or auto to the venue. You can also explore Jaipur metro options on Google map.

Check current metro timings and connections before travelling.

✈️ From Jaipur Airport, Jaipur ⌄

Take direct cab to the venue, Madhavam. (3.5 km)

Please check latest drivers or cab related local updates and routes before travelling.
